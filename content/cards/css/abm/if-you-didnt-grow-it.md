# "If you didn't grow it, you didn't explain it"

Joshua Epstein, who co-built Sugarscape, has been repeating that sentence since the late 1990s as the generativist's motto. His test for explaining a social pattern: could autonomous, boundedly rational agents interacting locally have produced it?

The bite is in what he refuses to say next. Epstein states it as a necessary condition only — "merely to generate is not necessarily to explain" — because absurd rules can produce the right picture. Growing your result buys you a seat, not the argument.
