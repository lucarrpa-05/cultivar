# The economics Nobel is not one of the Nobels

Alfred Nobel's will named five prizes, and economics was not among them. The Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel was created in 1968, funded by Sweden's central bank on its 300th anniversary, and first awarded in 1969 to Ragnar Frisch and Jan Tinbergen for econometric modelling.

A member of the Nobel family has publicly called it a public-relations coup by economists. The first woman to win it, in 2009, was Elinor Ostrom — a political scientist.

## Recall
type: reveal
Q: Why does the distinction matter beyond pedantry?
A: Because the prize's prestige is borrowed, and it has shaped which questions count as central economics. Ostrom's win is the counterexample worth remembering: the work that took the prize in 2009 was fieldwork on how communities govern shared resources, done outside the discipline's own methods.
