# "I placed too much faith in underpowered studies"

*Thinking, Fast and Slow* (2011) presents social priming as settled. In February 2017 Ulrich Schimmack and colleagues published a statistical autopsy of that chapter's sources, titled "Reconstruction of a Train Wreck". Kahneman turned up in the comments days later:

"What the blog gets absolutely right is that I placed too much faith in underpowered studies."

His own first paper with Tversky, in 1971, had warned against trusting samples that are too small. "I failed to internalize its message."
