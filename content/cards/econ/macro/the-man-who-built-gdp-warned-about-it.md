# The man who built GDP put the warning in the same report

In the depths of the Depression, Congress had no idea how bad things were: there was no national income statistic. Simon Kuznets was commissioned to build one, and his 1934 report to the Senate is the ancestor of every GDP figure since.

In it he wrote: "The welfare of a nation can, therefore, scarcely be inferred from a measurement of national income as defined above."

Ninety years of arguments about growth and wellbeing are, in effect, footnotes to a caveat its own author printed on delivery.
