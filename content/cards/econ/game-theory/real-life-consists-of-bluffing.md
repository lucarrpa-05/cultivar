# "Chess is not a game"

Jacob Bronowski shared a wartime taxi with John von Neumann and asked about his theory of games. *The Ascent of Man* (1973) reports the answer:

"Chess is not a game. Chess is a well-defined form of computation… Real life consists of bluffing, of little tactics of deception, of asking yourself what is the other man going to think I mean to do."

Bronowski was recalling, not transcribing. But the distinction splits the field: perfect information is an algorithm problem; private information is game theory.
