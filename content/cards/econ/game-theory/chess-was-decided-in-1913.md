# Chess was decided in 1913, in a way that helps nobody

Ernst Zermelo proved in 1913 that in a finite two-player game of perfect information with no chance, either one player can force a win or both can force at least a draw. Chess qualifies — but only because the fifty-move and repetition rules make it finite. So exactly one of three statements is true: White can force a win, Black can, or perfect play draws. Nobody knows which. The proof establishes that an answer exists and offers no way at all to find it.
