# The princess who asked Descartes how minds move bodies

On 6 May 1643, Elisabeth of Bohemia asked Descartes a question his mind–body dualism made urgent: how can an unextended mind move an extended body? Physical pushes involve size, contact and motion; his mind appeared to have none of these. Descartes replied by appealing to the union of mind and body that we experience in ordinary life. Elisabeth kept asking how that union could produce motion. Their letters turned a pointed objection into the **interaction problem**. Naming two kinds of substance does not yet explain how one acts on the other. Her question still tests attempts to defend this form of dualism.

## Recall
type: reveal
Q: What is the interaction problem?
A: If mind is unextended and matter is extended, there is no account of how one acts on the other — contact, force and direction all presuppose extension. Elisabeth of Bohemia put it to Descartes in 1643 and never got a satisfying reply.
