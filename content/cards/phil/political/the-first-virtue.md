# Justice is the first virtue of social institutions

> "Justice is the first virtue of social institutions, as truth is of systems of thought."

John Rawls, opening *A Theory of Justice* (1971), page 3.

The analogy is the argument in miniature. An elegant theory that is false gets thrown out, however elegant. Rawls says an efficient, prosperous, well-loved institution that is unjust should go the same way — and the rest of the book is an attempt to say what "unjust" means precisely enough to hold that line.
