# All models are wrong, but some are useful

> "Essentially, all models are wrong, but some are useful."

George Box and Norman Draper, *Empirical Model-Building and Response Surfaces* (Wiley, 1987), page 424. The thought runs through Box's 1976 paper in the *Journal of the American Statistical Association* too, where he warns against chasing correctness by adding elaboration.

The second clause is the working half. It replaces "is this model true?", which always answers no, with "is it wrong in a way that matters for what I am about to do with it?"
