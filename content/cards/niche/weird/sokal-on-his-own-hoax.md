# "Intellectually lazy"

In May 1996 *Social Text* ran "Transgressing the Boundaries: Towards a Transformative Hermeneutics of Quantum Gravity". Three weeks later its author, the NYU physicist Alan Sokal, revealed in *Lingua Franca* that it was nonsense, written in the vocabulary the editors liked.

> The results of my little experiment demonstrate, at the very least, that some fashionable sectors of the American academic Left have been getting intellectually lazy.

The journal was not peer-reviewed then, which both sides still argue about. His sharpest point survives it: flattery is a hole in every filter.
