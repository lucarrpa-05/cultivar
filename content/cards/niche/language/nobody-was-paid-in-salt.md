# The salt in your salary is a rumour

Everyone has heard it: legionaries were paid in salt, hence *salarium*, hence "worth his salt". Half of that is true. The Latin *salarium* is genuinely built on *sal*, salt — that part is not in doubt.

The practice is. No ancient source records soldiers being paid in salt, or even given a salt allowance; the claim that they were is described in reference works as baseless. What the money had to do with salt, nobody actually knows.

A word can preserve a connection perfectly and lose the reason for it entirely.

## Recall
type: reveal
Q: Which half of the "paid in salt" story survives scrutiny?
A: The etymology. *Salarium* is built on *sal* (salt) — but no ancient source attests soldiers being paid in salt, or receiving a salt ration. The link is real; the explanation is folk history.
