# Did Vermeer use a lens?

No surviving document says Vermeer owned a camera obscura. Yet some paintings show bright, soft-edged highlights that resemble optical effects, and Philip Steadman reconstructed room layouts consistent with a camera's viewpoint. In *Tim's Vermeer* (2013), Tim Jenison used a mirror comparator to make a striking imitation of *The Music Lesson*. That demonstrates a possible method, not Vermeer's actual one. Technical studies also find conventional perspective construction in his paintings. A lens might have helped him see, compose or transfer an image; none of those possibilities explains away his choices of colour, light and subject. The evidence remains an art-historical argument, not a solved trick.
