# Seventeen minutes before the bridge goes dark

Four people need to cross a narrow bridge at night. They take 1, 2, 5, and 10 minutes to cross. At most two can be on the bridge; a pair moves at the slower person's pace. They have one torch, and whoever crosses must carry it. The torch lasts 17 minutes.

Sending the one-minute person back and forth sounds sensible. Try it: every return is cheap, but the slow people make separate trips. Can you get everyone across before the light goes out? List each crossing and add the minutes. The trick is to spend a return trip to save a much larger forward trip.

## Recall
type: reveal
Q: What sequence gets all four across in 17 minutes?
A: The 1- and 2-minute people cross (2); 1 returns (1); 5 and 10 cross together (10); 2 returns (2); 1 and 2 cross (2). Total: 17.
