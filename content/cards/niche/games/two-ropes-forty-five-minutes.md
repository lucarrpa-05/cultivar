# Two ropes, no clock, forty-five minutes

Each of two ropes takes exactly one hour to burn from one end. Their thickness varies, so half a rope need not burn in half an hour. You have matches and need to measure 45 minutes. You may light either end of either rope, even while it is already burning.

A knot at the midpoint gives you no reliable time mark. Instead, ask what happens if fire eats a rope from *both* ends. Once you have a 30-minute event, you can make the second rope's remaining burn time shrink twice as fast. How would you arrange the lighting?

## Recall
type: reveal
Q: How do the two ropes mark 45 minutes despite burning unevenly?
A: Light rope A at both ends and rope B at one end. A finishes after 30 minutes. Then light B's other end. B had 30 minutes of one-ended burn time left; from both ends it finishes in 15 more minutes.
