# Your shower curtain can start its own vortex

Turn on the shower and a light curtain may lean inward. A common explanation invokes warm air rising; another points to moving air and pressure. In a fluid-dynamics simulation, David Schmidt found that the spray could set up a horizontal vortex with a low-pressure center that draws the curtain in. The work won a 2001 Ig Nobel Prize for a *partial* explanation. Which mechanism dominates can depend on the stall, spray and temperature, so the effect is a useful reminder that a familiar observation can have several interacting causes. Try cold water or a weighted hem and watch what changes.
