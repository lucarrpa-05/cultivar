# Your popcorn purchase reveals a little about your ticket

Why is cinema popcorn so expensive? One economic answer is **metering**: charge everyone for entry, then charge extra to customers who choose concessions. Researchers using sales from Spanish cinemas found that buyers of high-priced snacks tended to have higher willingness to pay for admission, consistent with this kind of price discrimination. That does not prove every popcorn buyer loves the film more, or that all ticket prices would rise without snack sales. Costs, competition and studio contracts matter too. But it explains why a seller might prefer a modest entry price paired with an expensive optional extra: customers reveal some of their willingness to spend by choosing it.
