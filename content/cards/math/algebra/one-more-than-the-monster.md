# The coincidence that was worth a Fields Medal

In 1978 John McKay noticed that 196884, a coefficient in the expansion of the modular $j$-function, is exactly one more than 196883, the smallest dimension in which the Monster group — the largest sporadic simple group, with about $8\times10^{53}$ elements — can be faithfully represented. Conway called it moonshine, meaning nonsense, and then he and Norton conjectured a whole dictionary in 1979. Richard Borcherds proved it in 1992 and took the 1998 Fields Medal for it.
