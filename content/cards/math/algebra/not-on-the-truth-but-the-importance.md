# What Galois asked for, the night before the duel

On 29 May 1832 Galois wrote to his friend Auguste Chevalier. Near the end he asked one thing of him, about the manuscripts he was leaving behind:

> "Ask Jacobi or Gauss publicly to give their opinion, not as to the truth, but as to the importance of these theorems."

In the original: *non sur la vérité, mais sur l'importance des théorèmes.*

He was twenty, and the Académie had turned him away three times. He did not ask to be believed. He asked to be read.
