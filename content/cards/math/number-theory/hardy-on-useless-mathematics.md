# Hardy's proudest boast, aged badly

"No one has yet discovered any warlike purpose to be served by the theory of numbers or relativity, and it seems very unlikely that anyone will do so for many years."

G. H. Hardy, *A Mathematician's Apology*, 1940: a defence of pure mathematics by a man who treated uselessness as a moral virtue.

Five years later, Hiroshima. Then RSA, and number theory became the only thing standing between a stranger and your bank account. Hardy was not careless — he was predicting the future, which mathematicians do badly.
