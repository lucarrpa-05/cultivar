# Nine mathematicians will advise AI labs on how to release proofs

On Monday 21 September 2026, nine mathematicians, including Martin Hairer, Timothy Gowers and Edward Witten, announced AGMAI, the Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence. OpenAI had approached some of them about an advisory board; they formed an independent, unpaid group instead, with technical support from the Institute for Advanced Study. First job: advising OpenAI on releasing significant results it credits to its internal model.

If you want an academic career, this is the fight over credit. Posting on Tao's blog the next day, Hairer wrote that recent lab releases paired high-profile results with "abject levels of scholarship."

Caveat: the role is advisory. Hairer concedes the labs can ignore it, and that nine people are not a representative sample.
