# Scraped off, prayed over, read again

A tenth-century scribe copied Archimedes onto parchment. In 1229 the pages were scraped, turned sideways, and reused for a prayer book. The old ink was faint, but it had not vanished.

Johan Heiberg recognised Archimedes beneath the prayers in 1906. After decades out of scholarly reach, the book resurfaced at a 1998 auction and later went to Baltimore for imaging. It preserves the only known copy of *The Method*, where Archimedes reveals a way he discovered results: imagine a shape sliced into thin pieces and balance them against pieces of a known shape on a lever. He then supplied geometric proofs, because the mechanical picture suggested an answer without establishing it.

## Recall
type: reveal
Q: Why does *The Method* matter more than the theorems in it?
A: *The Method* shows Archimedes using mechanical balance and sliced shapes to find results. He distinguished that discovery device from the geometric proof needed to establish each result.
