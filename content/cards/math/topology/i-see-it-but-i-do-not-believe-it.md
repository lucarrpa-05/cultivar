# Je le vois, mais je ne le crois pas

In 1877 Cantor wrote to Dedekind that he had built a one-to-one correspondence between the points of a line segment and the points of an $n$-dimensional cube. Then, mid-letter, he switched to French: "Je le vois, mais je ne le crois pas." I see it, but I do not believe it.

Dimension had just lost its grip on counting. What it had not lost was continuity: no such bijection can be continuous both ways, and proving that took another thirty-four years.
