# Gödel found a loophole in the Constitution. Einstein drove.

On 5 December 1947 Gödel went to his US citizenship hearing with two witnesses: Einstein and the economist Oskar Morgenstern. In the car he explained that he had found an internal inconsistency in the Constitution allowing the country to become a dictatorship legally. Both men begged him to keep quiet. Asked in the hearing whether such a thing could happen in America, Gödel began to explain. The judge, who had sworn Einstein in years before, cut him off and moved on. He passed.

## Recall
type: reveal
Q: What exactly was the flaw Gödel claimed to have found?
A: Nobody knows. Morgenstern's memorandum, written decades later, records the scene but not the argument, and Gödel never published it. Every reconstruction since — usually pointing at Article V, which lets the amendment procedure amend itself — is somebody's guess, not Gödel's.
