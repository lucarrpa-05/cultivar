# The weak force is not weak

By its basic coupling, the weak force is at least as strong as electromagnetism; by one standard measure, almost five times stronger. It looks feeble because of its messenger: the W boson weighs about 86 protons. A decaying neutron has about 1.3 MeV to spend, and a real W costs 80,000. So the decay runs through a W that never becomes real, and a free neutron lasts about fifteen minutes. Neutrinos, which feel only this force and gravity, cross the whole Earth for the same reason.
