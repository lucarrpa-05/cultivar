# The eight minutes that cost astronomy its circles

Kepler fitted circles to Tycho's Mars data for years. The best missed by eight minutes of arc — a quarter the width of the full Moon. He could have called it error.

In *Astronomia nova* (1609): "Now, because they could not be disregarded, these eight minutes alone will lead us along a path to the reform of the whole of Astronomy, and they are the matter for a great part of this work."

They did. The residual you decide to trust is the one that pays.
