# A species that vanished 40,000 years ago is still inside you

Svante Pääbo's group published a draft Neanderthal genome in 2010, read from bone powder. People with ancestry outside Africa carry a small Neanderthal fraction — the first estimate was one to four per cent, and current figures cluster near two. It is not inert filler: some of those inherited variants affect immunity and skin. Pääbo took the 2022 Nobel for making dead DNA readable at all.

## Recall
type: reveal
Q: What does a couple of per cent of Neanderthal DNA in living people actually demonstrate?
A: That the two populations interbred, and that some of the resulting variants were kept. Interbreeding happened; the descendants are us.
