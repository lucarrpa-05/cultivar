# The feather that made Darwin sick

Writing to Asa Gray on 3 April 1860, four months after the *Origin*:

> "The sight of a feather in a peacock's tail, whenever I gaze at it, makes me sick!"

A tail that heavy, that bright, that expensive should have been deleted by natural selection. His answer arrived in 1871: a second kind of selection, on mates rather than on survival. If peahens prefer the tail, the tail pays — even as its owner pays for it.
