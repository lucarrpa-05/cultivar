# The most under-stated sentence in the history of biology

Watson and Crick's paper in *Nature* on 25 April 1953 fills about a page. Two chains wind around a common axis; adenine always faces thymine, guanine always faces cytosine. Then, rather than claim anything, they close:

> "It has not escaped our notice that the specific pairing we have postulated immediately suggests a possible copying mechanism for the genetic material."

Pull the strands apart and each one is a mould for the other. The shape *was* the mechanism.
