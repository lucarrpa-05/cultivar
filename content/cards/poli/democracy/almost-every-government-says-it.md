# Almost every government on earth calls itself a democracy

The German Democratic Republic built a wall to stop its own citizens leaving. The Democratic People's Republic of Korea is a hereditary monarchy with missiles. Neither dropped the word, and neither thought that was funny.

That is not only cynicism, it is a clue. W. B. Gallie in 1956 called this kind of word an *essentially contested concept*: everyone wants it, so everyone defines it in a way they already satisfy. And the disagreement is not new. Athens filled most public offices by lot and treated elections as an aristocratic trick, on the grounds that a lottery cannot be bought. Robert Dahl, in 1971, thought no real state reaches the ideal at all, and coined *polyarchy* for what states actually manage: contested elections, plural sources of information, freedom to organise. Same word, opposite institutions.

So strip it back to the part nobody disputes. Whatever else democracy is, the majority decides. Hold that thought for one episode, because it is about to come apart in your hands.
