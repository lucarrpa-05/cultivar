# Where the handful of frontier labs came from

None of them started rich. Each was a few people with a thesis, and each found out the same thing: testing the thesis costs more than a university department can spend.

DeepMind was founded in London in 2010 by Demis Hassabis, Shane Legg and Mustafa Suleyman, and sold to Google in January 2014 for a price reported between \$400 and \$650 million, with an AI ethics board written into the deal. OpenAI was announced in December 2015 as a non-profit with \$1 billion pledged, founded by a group including Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Greg Brockman, Ilya Sutskever, John Schulman and Wojciech Zaremba. Anthropic was founded in 2021 by seven people who had left OpenAI, among them Dario and Daniela Amodei. In April 2023 Google folded its Brain team and DeepMind into one organisation.

The shape repeats, and it often repeats as a split: Anthropic's seven founders came out of OpenAI.

Which is what the phrase actually means. "Frontier lab" is a statement about capital and access to chips, not about who has the best ideas. The ideas are mostly published.
