# Some of Claude's principles came from Apple's terms of service

In May 2023 Anthropic published the document its model is trained against. The principles are drawn from the UN Declaration of Human Rights, from trust-and-safety practice, and from rules proposed by other labs, including DeepMind's Sparrow principles. For the mundane cases a 1948 human-rights declaration never anticipated — personal data, impersonation, identity — they drew on what they called global platform guidelines, naming Apple's terms of service. A further set was written to push the model away from assuming a Western, rich, industrialised point of view.

## Recall
type: reveal
Q: Why did a human-rights declaration need supplementing with a phone company's fine print?
A: Because most of what a chat model has to get right is mundane — impersonation, data, harassment — and platform terms are decades of accumulated practice on exactly those cases.
