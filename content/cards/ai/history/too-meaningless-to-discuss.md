# Turing thought his own question was meaningless

He opens with "I propose to consider the question, 'Can machines think?'" Six sections later, in *Mind*, October 1950:

> The original question, "Can machines think?" I believe to be too meaningless to deserve discussion.

His reason is not modesty. "Machine" and "think" cannot be defined without smuggling the answer in, so he replaced the question with a game that has a score. Seventy-odd years of arguing about whether a program *really* understands is an argument about the exact thing Turing refused to have.
