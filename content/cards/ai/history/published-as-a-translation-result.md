# The transformer was published as a translation result

"Attention Is All You Need", posted 12 June 2017, is a machine-translation paper. Its headline numbers are BLEU scores: 28.4 on WMT 2014 English-to-German, 41.8 English-to-French, after 3.5 days on eight GPUs. The pitch is efficiency — same quality, far less training. Eight authors share a footnote reading "Equal contribution. Listing order is random." Nothing in the abstract mentions language modelling, chatbots or general capability.

## Recall
type: reveal
Q: What was the 2017 transformer paper claiming?
A: A better speed/quality tradeoff on machine translation — 28.4 BLEU on English-to-German in 3.5 days on eight GPUs. Everything the architecture later became was downstream of a translation benchmark.
