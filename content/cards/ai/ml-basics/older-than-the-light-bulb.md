# Gradient descent is older than the light bulb

Augustin-Louis Cauchy published the method in 1847, in a short note to the French Academy of Sciences: to attack a system of equations too nasty to solve outright, compute the derivative, take a small step downhill, repeat. He was after astronomical calculations, not neurons. Edison's practical bulb arrived thirty-two years later. Every model you have heard of is trained by a procedure Cauchy would recognise on sight.

Q: How old is the algorithm that trains every modern neural network?
A: Cauchy described gradient descent in 1847 for astronomical computations. The 21st-century additions are the stochastic mini-batch, momentum, and per-coordinate step sizes — refinements on a 19th-century idea.
