# The statistician who told statistics it was 2% of the story

Leo Breiman, thirteen years a consultant before Berkeley, in *Statistical Science*, 2001:

> "There are two cultures in the use of statistical modeling to reach conclusions from data. One assumes that the data are generated by a given stochastic data model. The other uses algorithmic models and treats the data mechanism as unknown."

He put the first culture at 98% of statisticians. His own random forests were in the other 2%. That 2% is now an industry — and his warning, that accuracy is not understanding, got skipped.
