# The World Cup was found by a dog called Pickles

On 20 March 1966, four months before England hosted the World Cup, the Jules Rimet Trophy was stolen from a stamp exhibition at Westminster Central Hall. Scotland Yard had nothing.

On 27 March a black-and-white collie called Pickles stopped at a hedge in Beulah Hill, South London, and would not move. Under it, wrapped in newspaper, was the trophy. His owner collected nearly £5,000 — enough to buy a house. Pickles got a year of free dog food, a film role, and an invitation to England's victory banquet.
