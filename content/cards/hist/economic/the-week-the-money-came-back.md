# Remember bank runs? Here is one broken in a week

Remember how a bank run can become self-fulfilling? In March 1933, Americans withdrew cash because they feared other depositors would do the same. Even a bank with sound loans could run out of ready money.

Roosevelt took office on 4 March. Two days later he declared a nationwide bank holiday. Congress passed an emergency law on 9 March; on 12 March Roosevelt used his first fireside chat to explain that only examined banks would reopen. They began reopening the next day. Americans returned about \$1 billion to banks in the following week.

The pause, examination and public explanation changed what depositors expected from one another. That was the hinge.
