# Take nobody's word for it

The Royal Society was founded on 28 November 1660 and chartered in 1662. Its motto, taken from Horace, is *Nullius in verba* — take nobody's word for it. The point was not scepticism as a pose but a procedure: settle questions by experiment rather than by citing an authority, including this one. In 1665 it launched *Philosophical Transactions*, the first journal given over entirely to science, and with it the habit of publishing so that strangers can check you.

## Recall
type: reveal
Q: What does *Nullius in verba* commit the Royal Society to?
A: Deciding questions by experiment instead of by authority — literally "take nobody's word for it", including the Society's own. The founding of *Philosophical Transactions* in 1665 made the checking public.
