# There was no such thing, and this is a book about it

> "There was no such thing as the Scientific Revolution, and this is a book about it."

Steven Shapin, opening *The Scientific Revolution* (University of Chicago Press, 1996). He is not denying Kepler, Galileo, Boyle or Newton. He is refusing a label that bundles unlike people into one movement with one method — a period name coined long afterwards.

It bites because every field keeps one. The Enlightenment. The Industrial Revolution. The AI revolution. Convenient containers that hide how accidental the contents were.
