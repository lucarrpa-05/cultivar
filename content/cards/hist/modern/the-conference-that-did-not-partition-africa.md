# The conference that did not partition Africa

Bismarck hosted fourteen delegations in Berlin from November 1884 to February 1885; no African state was represented. The meeting recognized the Congo Free State's governing association and set Congo basin trade rules. It stated vague rules for new coastal claims.

It did not partition the continent. A 2024 study finds that the conference dealt with Congo frontiers, most revised. The median African colonial border reached its final form in 1908. The classroom map compresses decades of conquest and negotiation into one room, making the violence too easy to miss.
