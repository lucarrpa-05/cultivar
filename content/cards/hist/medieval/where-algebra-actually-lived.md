# Remember al-Khwarizmi? Now meet the argument about his address

Remember al-Khwarizmi, whose name gave us *algorithm*? He is often pictured at Baghdad's House of Wisdom, a grand academy under the caliph al-Ma'mun. Historian Dimitri Gutas disputes that familiar building: contemporary references are sparse, and major translators do not say they worked at such an academy. Other historians disagree with his skeptical reading.

The wider translation movement is not in doubt. Over generations, patrons in Abbasid Baghdad paid scholars to render Greek and Syriac mathematics, medicine and philosophy into Arabic. The interesting question is where that work actually happened: perhaps in a library, certainly across a city of scholars and patrons. One memorable building can obscure the network that made the books travel.
