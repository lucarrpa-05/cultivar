# The students hired the professors, and fined them

A *universitas* was not a campus. It was a guild, and at Bologna the guild was made of students — foreigners who banded together for legal protection. An elected student council decided what professors were paid and whether they were kept, and could fine a professor who failed to finish the course material on time. The enforcement mechanism was the student strike. Bologna claims 1088, a date reconstructed by a committee of historians in the nineteenth century, and disputed since.
